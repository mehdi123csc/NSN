NSN WORLD 3D — PHASE 1B: NSN ICONS

This update adds four original fictional fashion personas to the 3D studio:
- Luna Veyra — elegance
- Zayn Noir — luxury streetwear
- Amara Sol — bold style
- Kai Sterling — premium sport

The personas are original brand characters, not digital replicas of real celebrities.
The current meshes remain procedural Three.js geometry. They have persona-specific skin/hair colors and labels, but they are NOT realistic scanned human models and do not yet use rigged GLB/GLTF assets, facial animation, or cloth simulation.

No Supabase credentials are included. Product/order persistence is still local/demo until the owner connects Supabase and configures secure database policies.
